# Tagging strategy

Status: Operational

## Principle

A tag is an earned editorial signal, not a notification for every ledger change and not a reach hack.

Tag a pundit in an original post only when Pundits.Pro is giving that person one of three things worth receiving: a stage in a notable pregame debate, specific credit for a notable correct call, or recognition of a meaningful tracked-record milestone. The tagged person must be central to the copy and visible in the attached Tier 1 card.

Routine capture and routine accountability continue without tags. The ledger records hits and misses evenly; the account does not push a miss into a pundit's notifications.

This policy governs original posts. A reply naturally addresses the account whose thread it enters and continues to follow `reply-guide.md`.

## The three earned-tag moments

### 1. Roll Call — a heavily contested game

Tag before kickoff when all of these are true:

- `kind` is `game`;
- at least four distinct pundits are mapped to the event;
- both sides are represented; and
- the post is about the group disagreement, not one isolated pick.

Lead with the density and split: how many tracked pundits, how each side divides, and why this is the contested game in the book. Use the event card and post at most one tagged Roll Call per event.

Tag no more than four pundits in the original post and include at least one voice from each side. Tag only pundits named in the copy or clearly identified on the card. If more than four approved handles qualify, prioritize the opposing voices featured in the copy and pundits the account has not tagged during the previous seven ET days. The card and copy may name the complete group; do not create extra replies merely to notify the rest.

If the game has fewer than four pundits or only one voice on a side, use The Disagreement or The Slate without tags.

### 2. Flowers — a notable correct call

Tag the pundit when a mapped take grades `hit`, the call was clear and specific, and at least one of these makes the credit socially meaningful:

- it was the minority or underdog side at the frozen price;
- it decided the winning side of a Roll Call-eligible game;
- it was attached to the week's marquee event; or
- the call itself was unusually precise and the result directly validated it.

Give credit for the call, not a general claim of expertise. State the frozen price and result. A Flowers post replaces an ordinary hit Receipt; it does not add another post about the same result.

The pundit tag belongs in the original post. An approved outlet handle may also be tagged only when the post explicitly credits the originating show or outlet and that outlet is material to the evidence. Do not add outlet tags for generic reach.

Never tag a pundit in a miss Receipt.

### 3. Milestone — a meaningful tracked record

Tag a pundit when the current ledger supports a positive or neutral milestone that would still be meaningful with the sample size printed beside it. Initial eligible milestones are:

- 5th, 10th, or 25th graded pick;
- 5th, 10th, or 25th hit;
- at least three consecutive graded hits; or
- sole possession of the leaderboard lead with at least five graded picks.

Always say `tracked record`, `on record`, or `on Pundits.Pro`, and show wins, losses, and graded sample size when the post makes a performance claim. A 1-0 start, a one-pick lead, or a pending-pick count is not a performance milestone.

Negative milestones can support an untagged Number or ordinary ledger post when editorially useful. Do not tag someone to deliver a negative milestone.

## Do not tag

- A routine Freeze or newly captured pick.
- An ordinary Receipt, especially a miss.
- A one-person preview or thin one-sided event.
- Tiny-sample leaderboard movement.
- A repeated post about the same pundit and event.
- A person who is not central to the copy and card.
- An outlet merely because it has a large audience.
- Any handle not listed below. Spell out the pundit's name instead; never guess a handle.

## Measurement

Tagging is a relationship and amplification experiment, not assumed algorithmic distribution. Review each tagged original by earned moment: Roll Call, Flowers, or Milestone.

Track:

- organic views and public engagements;
- whether a tagged pundit or outlet replies, reposts, or quote-posts;
- profile visits, follows from the post, and URL clicks when private analytics are available;
- referral traffic from covered pundits or outlets when measurable; and
- tag concentration, so one pundit is not repeatedly used as the account's distribution channel.

Record paid or boosted reach separately. It cannot demonstrate that a tag or archetype worked organically.
