# News beats (Scout hunt map)

Status: Operational

Date: 2026-08-29

News Scout owns **bylined articles and expert-pick pages**. Shows Scout owns video, podcasts, TV clips, and archived sports-radio audio. X Scout owns status URLs.

Open the page. Name the speaker. SU means the person picks the winner of a Dispatch game. “No Pick” on a grid is Dropped with the opened URL. Unnamed staff consensus is Dropped. A paywall or snippet without the full evidence is Dropped.

Hunt only sports that appear on today’s `## Dispatch`. Skip `dense` rows unless a page already open names that game.
