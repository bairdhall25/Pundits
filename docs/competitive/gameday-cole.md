# Cole's Gameday Blog Competitive Profile

Status: Evidence with an active monitoring checklist

Baseline researched: 2026-08-29

Website: https://gamedaycole.com/

X: [@gamedaycole](https://x.com/gamedaycole)

Builder: Cole Reagan, operating as a fan project

## Current assessment

Cole's Gameday Blog is the closest operating analog to Pundits.Pro's core job: capture named college-football analysts' public picks, keep a record, and grade them. It is a long-running one-person blog with a real historical corpus and almost no product, brand, or distribution surface.

It is the incumbent Pundits should respect on CFB analyst records. It is not a reason to copy the spreadsheet aesthetic, add ATS unit P&L as the primary object, or treat GameDay-only coverage as the whole market. Pundits should compete on receipts (quote, source, frozen price), event-level disagreement, NFL as well as CFB, and X/search distribution.

## Baseline signals

- Site tagline: "Tracking college football picks and predictions for YOU."
- About page: Cole is unaffiliated with ESPN or College GameDay. He tracks "expert predictions week to week with focus on College Gameday, but also with as many analysts as I can fit in."
- NCAA.com profiled Cole Reagan on 2018-08-31 as the person who tracks Lee Corso's GameDay picks. He started in 2009 (legal pad, then spreadsheet) after watching Corso pick beside Chipper Jones. He works in IT in Dallas.
- Sporting News still pointed readers to gamedaycole.com in 2025 for GameDay celebrity guest-picker records, and told readers to follow `@gamedaycole`.
- X profile on 2026-08-29 (fxtwitter): display name Cole, 3,489 followers, 791 following, 8,690 posts. Bio: "Tracking college football picks for the good of humanity."
- Related accounts: `@gamedayjen` (Jennifer, 87 followers) and `@Thegamedaybaby` (26 followers).
- Homepage on 2026-08-29 already had the new season's first Cover 3 LOCKS table (Bud Elliott, Chip Patterson, Danny Kanell, Tom Fornelli ATS and money-line sprinkles) plus full 2025-26 graded SU / ATS / ML standings.
- 2025-26 SU champion on the site: Tom Fornelli 254-75 (77.2%). ATS unit leader: Collin Wilson (Action Network). The tables include Herbstreit, Kanell, Patterson, Fallica, McAfee, Saban, Finebaum, and many others.
- Support: Patreon (https://www.patreon.com/gamedaycole). Data-requests page offers CSVs if GamedayCole.com is credited; email gamedaycole@gmail.com or Twitter DM.
- No public company, funding, app, or paid consumer product was verified beyond Patreon and data requests.

### Capture rules (from About; verified copy)

1. Track winner predictions, straight up or against the spread.
2. ATS underdog (taking points) does not count as an SU winner pick; laying points as a favorite does.
3. If an expert flips, the most recent pick is official.
4. Only count clear first-person language ("I'm picking", "I'll take", "I like", "I'm going with"). Unclear analysis is an under-pick.
5. Sample-size rule ("Matt Millen rule") before crowning a champion.

## Competitive interpretation

### Strengths

- Years of CFB analyst history, including GameDay and Cover 3, that Pundits has not built.
- Operating this week: new-season LOCKS already logged.
- Citation gravity: NCAA.com and Sporting News treat him as the record.
- Explicit first-person pick standard that is close to Pundits' mapped-pick rule.
- Willingness to share data as CSV.

### Weaknesses

- Blog + tables, not a fan product. No event cards, no quote-as-object, no frozen market, no hit/miss story URLs.
- Tiny X relative to the cultural job he does.
- CFB-centric; no verified NFL product surface.
- ATS/unit P&L sits beside SU records, which pulls the site toward gambling-tracker rather than receipts-for-fans.
- One operator; coverage is whatever Cole can watch and type.
- No visible search/social card system.

## Implications for Pundits.Pro

- Do not pretend the "who is picking what, and were they right?" job is empty. Cole has been doing the CFB half since 2009.
- Do not try to beat him first on lifetime Corso headgear trivia. Beat him on the object: quote, source URL, freeze, event split, permanent URL, grade, card.
- GameDay and Cover 3 are shared capture sources, not competitors.
- A historical corpus is a real gap. If Cole's CSV terms are clean, that is cheaper than reconstructing 15 years of GameDay from YouTube.
- Keep empty sides honest. Cole's under-pick rule is the same instinct as Pundits' "do not stretch a pick."

## Partnership thesis

Cole could be a data or history partner, not an acquisition target in any practical sense. Possible arrangements: credited CSV license for historical GameDay/analyst SU records; reciprocal links; he remains the GameDay historian while Pundits is the receipt product.

Do not pursue until:

- Data rights and credit terms are written down.
- A sample CSV is auditable against known GameDay weeks.
- Integration cost is lower than capturing current season ourselves (current season should stay first-party).

## Monitoring checklist

- Homepage still logging weekly LOCKS / GameDay picks during the 2026 season.
- Follower count and whether posts are tables vs. cards.
- New sports, NFL, or a real app/UI.
- Patreon or data-license changes.
- Citations from ESPN, NCAA.com, Sporting News, or similar.
- Whether he starts attaching source clips or market prices.
