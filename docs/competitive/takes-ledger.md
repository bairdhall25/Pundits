# Takes Ledger Competitive Profile

Status: Evidence with an active monitoring checklist

Baseline researched: 2026-08-31

Website: https://www.takesledger.com/

Public product name: Receipts

Team or builder: Not identified in the reviewed public product surfaces

## Current assessment

Takes Ledger is a direct but narrow analogue to Pundits.Pro. Its live product tracks Denver Broncos takes, organizes video and article predictions, shows a pundit leaderboard, maps writer picks across the team schedule, and offers a weekly email signup.

The site is operating and its media feed was current at the baseline, but it had not demonstrated the grading loop: every visible pundit record was ungraded and displayed no accuracy. The apparent Broncos-only scope, small corpus, absent public methodology, and unfinished search metadata make it a low current threat. Its team-specific focus and schedule view are worth monitoring because they test whether a local fan identity can support a tighter repeat-use product than a league-wide ledger.

## Baseline signals

- The homepage described the product as `Receipts` and `The Denver Broncos takes ledger`.
- The leaderboard listed 11 recognizable national pundits. Six had at least one take; Skip Bayless led the visible count with three.
- All visible records were `0 / 0 / 0`, with accuracy shown as `—`, so no settled prediction or public grading cadence was yet demonstrated.
- Pundit detail pages included take text, positive/neutral/negative sentiment, and time-linked YouTube clip sources.
- The media page showed nine videos dated August 25–29, 2026, across shows including Rich Eisen, First Things First, The Herd, and Mina Kimes.
- The schedule page mapped writer picks game by game for the 2026 Broncos season and separated `Pundit takes` from `Writer picks`.
- The homepage listed six article-prediction sources, including NFL.com, FanSided, ClutchPoints, and Mile High Report. Sample article detail preserved the source link and a summarized season outlook.
- The footer offered a newsletter called `The Weekly Receipt`.
- No pricing, advertising, customer, revenue, funding, partnership, or team signal was visible on the reviewed product routes.
- The reviewed pages still used generic `Create Next App` metadata or description in several places. `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` returned 404 at the baseline.

## Competitive interpretation

### Strengths

- A clear local-team wedge that gives Broncos fans an immediate reason to care.
- Simple fan vocabulary: receipts, accuracy, takes, sentiment, and schedule.
- Fresh video discovery with recognizable personalities and direct clip links.
- A schedule view that turns season predictions into game-by-game disagreement.
- An email capture aligned with the product's likely weekly habit.

### Weaknesses

- No visible graded result, accuracy record, or proven close-the-loop cadence.
- Broncos-only scope and a small visible corpus.
- No public methodology, eligibility standard, objective grading rule, correction policy, or source-rights explanation was found on the reviewed routes.
- Some article records used generic staff attribution or lacked a visible publication date.
- Pundit take pages showed source clips but not a visible source publication date in the reviewed sample.
- No frozen market context or other measure of how bold a prediction was.
- Generic metadata and missing crawl files weaken trust, branding, and search discovery.
- No visible distribution, business model, team identity, or traction evidence beyond the product itself.

## Implications for Pundits.Pro

The strongest differentiator is not the existence of a leaderboard. Pundits.Pro should keep proving the full receipt: named source, verbatim evidence, frozen market context, explicit grading rule, fast result, and permanent record.

Takes Ledger does expose a useful strategic question: whether a team-first entry point creates more repeat use than a general picks feed. Monitor its Broncos schedule, newsletter, and local-source mix as evidence for or against stronger team landing pages and team-specific distribution. Do not react by narrowing Pundits.Pro to one team without engagement evidence.

## Partnership thesis

Partnership potential is currently unclear. If Takes Ledger develops durable Broncos and local-media coverage, it could become a complementary team-specific source or distribution partner. Any discussion should wait for evidence of reliable grading, clear source/data rights, identifiable ownership, and a corpus that is costly for Pundits.Pro to reproduce.

## Monitoring checklist

- First graded predictions and time from result to grade.
- Whether public records reconcile with individual take outcomes.
- Continued media freshness after the opening weeks of the NFL season.
- Growth beyond Broncos coverage or deeper ownership of the Broncos niche.
- Methodology, grading, correction, attribution, and source-rights disclosures.
- Newsletter cadence and visible audience or referral signals.
- Team, company, funding, customer, revenue, press, or partnership evidence.
- Search metadata, robots, sitemap, indexed pages, backlinks, and branded search visibility.
- Product changes that improve evidence quality, fan comprehension, or repeat use.
- Whether a supplier or local-distribution partnership becomes more credible.
