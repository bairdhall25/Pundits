## Current assessment

Tallysight is the closest *distribution* play to "who the experts are taking." They have spent years aggregating named-outlet predictions (ESPN, CBS, Athletic, Barstool) into a Rotten-Tomatoes-style Pulse, and the 2026 corporate site is a creator network that routes that participation into licensed prediction platforms including Kalshi.

They are not a quote+URL+freeze ledger. They are the company most likely to ship a Pundits-shaped card from an existing expert firehose. Watch Pulse this season more than Ledger.

## Baseline signals

- About page 2026-08-29 (self-reported): 7+ years in market; 50+ prediction platforms integrated; 1,500+ sports creators and media partners; platforms named include DraftKings, FanDuel, Kalshi, Polymarket. Claims of powering The Athletic, USA Today, Sportradar, Entain.
- LinkedIn: San Diego, founded ~2019, ~6 employees in one 2026 snapshot.
- X 2026-08-29 (fxtwitter): `@tallysight`, 4,202 followers, 14,447 posts. Bio: "Prediction infrastructure for sports creators. Monetize your audience."
- Substack Pulse (example Oct 2025, still on the 2026 site): "What 285 Experts Think Will Happen" — 7,416 NFL+CFB predictions, 285 creators; self-described "Rotten Tomatoes, but for sports predictions." Directs readers to tally.site to compare picks vs experts and "which creators are actually beating the market."
- Consumer tally.site was behind a Cloudflare challenge from this research box, so live 2026-08-29 rankings were not independently scraped. Treat Pulse plus about-page claims as the verified surface.
- Monetization is creator/platform infrastructure, not a public receipt book. No verbatim-quote product was verified.

## Competitive interpretation

### Strengths

- Scale of expert-consensus aggregation Pundits will not match this year.
- Already in the Kalshi/creator stack Pundits uses as a price source.
- Pulse is a weekly habit with a clear metaphor.
- Seven years of media-partner relationships.

### Weaknesses

- Consensus % is the object, not a named person's sentence.
- 2026 homepage is B2B ("route volume"), which can starve the fan product.
- X is small relative to the claimed creator network.
- Mixes team-site writers, handicappers, and TV talent into one "expert" blob.

## Implications for Pundits.Pro

- If someone wants "what 285 experts think," that is Tallysight. Pundits should stay "what Patterson said, with the tape and the freeze."
- Kalshi adjacency is a collision risk *and* a partnership door. They can put a creator betslip next to a Kalshi contract; Pundits can put a frozen cent next to a quote. Different layers, same weekend.
- Do not try to ingest 1,500 creators. Event density with a few verifiable voices is still the Pundits order of operations.

## Partnership thesis

Possible: Pundits as the receipt/quote layer on a Tallysight consensus card, or Tallysight as distribution into Kalshi. Only if Pulse still exists as a consumer habit and they want a sourced human rather than another creator CTA. High watch item. Do not assume they will stay a fan product.

## Monitoring checklist

- Is tally.site / Pulse still shipping weekly NFL+CFB consensus in the 2026 season?
- Any named-quote or source-URL objects, not just %.
- Kalshi campaign pages that look like Pundits fight cards.
- X cadence and follower growth.
- Whether the Athletic/USA Today widgets still show Tallysight branding.
