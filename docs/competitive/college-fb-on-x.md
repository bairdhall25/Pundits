## Current assessment

`@CollegeFBonX` is a mid-size CFB engagement account, not a pundit tracker. It matters because it is the live example of the card format that gets replies on X: 16:9 matchup graphic, score-prediction prompt, audience fills the thread.

Pundits already decided internally (2026-08-29) to steal the craft (two team tiles, kickoff, one number) and reject the bait (fan score predictions, team-color logo templates). Log it here so that decision has a named competitor, not a vibe.

A second 2026-08-29 handle hunt confirmed this is the real "CollegeFBonX" account. `@CFBonX` / `@CFBOnX` are not separate real accounts. `@CFBReport` and `@CFB247` are larger CFB graphic shops under different brands.

## Competitive interpretation

### Strengths

- Reply-native posts. A who-ya-got card is a conversation, which is what X currently ranks.
- Consistent visual system fans recognize in the feed.
- 50k+ followers is the scale `@Pundits_` does not have (3 followers on the same day).

### Weaknesses

- The content is fan score guesses, memes, and highlights. Nothing is a mapped pick.
- No grade, no source, no pundit, no market. The thread dies when the game ends.
- Easy to copy visually and still lose, which Pundits already did when a CollegeFBonX-style mock was rejected.

## Implications for Pundits.Pro

- Use this account as a distribution benchmark, not a product spec.
- A Pundits card in the same slot should ask a checkable question about *named people* ("Patterson 26¢ vs Finebaum 75¢ — somebody's going in the book"), not "what's your score."
- Reply Guy should keep hunting this account's question posts; that is inbound, not competition-copying.
- Never clone team-color tiles, navy texture, or a center athletic Vs. Brand lockup stays lime `PUNDITS.` on `#0A0A0A`.

## Partnership thesis

None unless they want to post a Pundits split as the "correct" answer under a score-prediction card. Do not pay for that until `@Pundits_` can fill the replies itself.

## Monitoring checklist

- Follower count and typical views on Week 0/1 matchup cards.
- Whether they add analyst names, odds, or grades to the graphics.
- Format changes (vertical vs 16:9, poll vs image).
- Copycats at similar follower scale.
- Whether `@collegefb_zone` on IG/TikTok starts carrying named-pundit content.
