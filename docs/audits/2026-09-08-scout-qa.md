## Required fixes

### P1 — Approved targets without public events disappear

`scripts/scout-targets-lib.mjs:86` filters approved targets by a nonempty eventSlug; `scripts/scout-density-lib.mjs:266` then iterates only public events. All four proposed college games have null eventSlug. Changing their state to approved in memory produces **four approved targets and zero college Dispatch rows**. Providing a future slug alone also cannot overcome iteration over existing events.

This blocks the intended capture-before-minting workflow: Scout needs a pick to justify a new public event, but cannot hunt the selected matchup until that event exists.

Required behavior: include approved unpublished matchups as first-class staging targets using stable target IDs, matchup, season, verified kickoff, priority, and designated sources. They must be huntable without writing data/events.json or inventing a public slug. Stage evidence with target/matchup context; Audit verifies it, and Promote retains the explicit event-mint gate. Update Coordinator and lane instructions as well as code. Test proposed -> approved -> Dispatch -> unmapped Intake with unchanged editorial JSON. Test expired and deferred unpublished targets too.

### P1 — Audit identity does not bind all approved evidence

`scripts/scout-handoff-lib.mjs:21` hashes pundit, event, side, quote, and source URL but omits sourceDate and reasoning. Changing either field after an `ok` verdict leaves approvalStillValid true and promoteReadyRows returns one ready row. Promote is told to copy approved reasoning, so an edited unsupported capsule can inherit an earlier approval.

Required behavior: bind the audited evidence version, including source date and optional rationale, or maintain separate explicitly checked approvals for them. A substantive edit must require re-audit. Preserve whitespace normalization where harmless. Do not invalidate approvals for run-only notes or later milestone timestamps. The legacy no-rowId fallback also omits sourceUrl; fail closed or provide an explicit migration rule for legacy records. Test date, rationale, URL, speaker, side, and quote changes through the actual handoff path.

### P2 — A hit or opened episode cannot be deliberately reopened

`scripts/scout-feeds-lib.mjs:227` unconditionally classifies inspected hit/opened episodes as inspected. Unlike dry episodes, it ignores reopenReason. With the real GMFB ledger entry, adding a reason for another designated speaker or newly approved target still returns inspected, excluding it from inspectableEpisodes.

Required behavior: honor a deliberate reopen reason for partial/hit/opened episodes, preserving previous evidence and completed coverage. Track enough target/segment coverage to distinguish one captured pick from completion of the source. Do not repeatedly reopen completed episodes. Test a new target on a previously productive episode and an additional speaker in a partially checked segment. This does not assert that a specific additional pick exists in the current GMFB episode.
