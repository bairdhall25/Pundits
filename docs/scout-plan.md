# Scout is the product

Status: Operational

Date: 2026-08-29.

The site cannot invent faces. Scout must land story-ready straight-up winner picks with durable evidence; Audit, Promote, UI, and Grader only close the loop on what Scout verifies.

## Goal

A fan opening pundits.pro should see **several named, sourced winner-picks on each homepage game**, NCAAF and NFL, preferably disagreement.

Success is qualified event density:

- `empty-side` — YES or NO has zero mapped hard SUs; urgent.
- `off-home` — a queued marquee game has no roster SU; find one so Promote can propose `onHome`.
- `thin` — both sides have a voice but total mapped hard SUs is under three; keep hunting.
- `dense` — at least three mapped hard SUs and both sides represented; done for the week.

Architecture: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-29-scout-architecture-design.md`. Dispatch is generated by `node scripts/scout-density.mjs`.

## Pipeline

1. Coordinator writes `## Dispatch` from live JSON.
2. Shows, X, and News append separate passes to the same `docs/runs/YYYY-MM-DD.md` mailbox.
3. Shows owns video, podcasts, TV clips, and bounded durable sports-radio archives.
4. Audit reopens every new hard source.
5. Promote ships only audited roster rows. Candidates are not auto-rostered.
6. The application derives permanent pick stories from promoted calls.

Scout never edits `data/`. Fantasy and props remain parked.

## Tokens and cost discipline

Do not stop at a shallow search because tokens feel scarce. Open the listed factory, jump the picking block, and try both teams. The scarce thing is a verified SU.

Budget is not permission for repeated low-yield sweeps. After the curated sources and two reasonable named searches per under-dense game are exhausted, record the miss and move on.

Radio is a two-week pilot inside the existing Shows job:

- no separate Radio Scout or additional routine;
- national rostered programs first, then at most two local archived programs per matchup;
- one bounded radio fallback per sport/pick window;
- log programs opened and results in `Radio coverage`;
- target no more than 10–15% incremental weekly Grok Bot usage and do not enable on-demand auto top-up for the pilot.

## Quality bar

- **SU:** the named person picks the winner of a listed game.
- **URL:** Audit can reopen the exact page, episode, clip, transcript, or status URL.
- **Speaker:** the quote belongs to the named person, never “the show” or “the station.”
- **Season:** the evidence is for this season’s event slug.
- **Photo:** required to roster, not to stage a Candidate.

YES remains the away team. Wrong year is Dropped. Futures map only to future slugs. ATS, totals, callers, polls, station consensus, live-only audio, and inaccessible snippets do not qualify.

## Cadence

- Coordinator: daily, cheap Dispatch refresh.
- Shows NCAAF: Thu–Sat and the Saturday pregame window.
- Shows NFL: Tue–Sat of that NFL week.
- Radio: within the applicable Shows pass only, after normal factories, once per pick window.
- X: twice daily, last 48 hours.
- News NCAAF: Thu–Sat.
- News NFL: Tue–Sat of that NFL week.
- Audit and Promote: only when the run contains new hard rows.

## What we will not do

- Auto-add pundits from Candidates.
- Add a separate radio bot before the pilot proves yield.
- Use callers, fan polls, anonymous consensus, or live-only audio.
- Invent quotes, infer a pick from analysis, or stretch titles onto games.
- Build group competition UI, a backend, or new-sport hunt maps in this pass.
